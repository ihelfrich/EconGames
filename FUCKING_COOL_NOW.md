## What Just Got Added

### 1. **Educational Overlay System** 🎓

**Real-time economic insights** that pop up as you play:

- **Concept Explanations**: Beautiful purple gradient boxes at the top explaining what's happening
- **Insight Notifications**: Sliding notifications from the right with:
  - 📈 Price changes ("Price Rising! More buyers than sellers!")
  - ✅ Market efficiency alerts ("Market Efficiency! Total surplus: $1,234")
  - ⚠️ Market imbalance warnings
  - 🎓 Deep economic insights when you switch modes
  - 💰 Trade value notifications ("Trade Creates Value! $12.50 in total surplus created!")

**Mode-specific teaching**:
- Switch to **Monopoly** → Instantly see: "Single seller controls the market! They restrict quantity to raise prices above marginal cost (P > MC). Watch consumer surplus shrink and deadweight loss appear! 📊"
- Then get the insight: "Monopolists produce where MR = MC, but charge price from the demand curve. The gap between P and MC is their market power."

### 2. **Price History Chart** 📊

**Bottom left corner** - Live price tracking over time:
- See how prices evolve as you adjust parameters
- Spot trends and market dynamics
- 100 data points of history
- Time stamps in seconds
- Beautiful line graph with gradient fill

### 3. **Enhanced Trade Visualization** 💰

Every trade now shows:
- **Price** (as before)
- **+$X.X surplus** - showing the VALUE CREATED by that specific trade
- This teaches that **trade creates value** - both parties win!

### 4. **Animated Notifications** ✨

All insights:
- **Slide in** from the right
- **Color-coded** by type:
  - Blue = Info
  - Orange = Warning
  - Green = Success
  - Purple = Economic Insight
- **Click to dismiss** anytime
- **Auto-fade** after 4-8 seconds
- **Stack vertically** so you can see multiple at once

## How It Actually Works Now

### When You Launch:
1. Beautiful market environment loads
2. Agents spawn with idle animations
3. Chart starts building from real data
4. **First insight pops up**: "🎓 Economic Insight: In perfect competition, no single buyer or seller can influence price - they are 'price takers.' This leads to allocative efficiency!"

### When You Switch to Monopoly:
1. **Big purple concept box**: "💡 Monopoly Power" with full explanation
2. One second later: **Insight notification** with game theory details
3. Chart updates to show restricted supply curve
4. Trades become less frequent
5. Prices rise
6. **Alert**: "Price Rising! 📈 Sellers gaining market power!"

### When You Adjust Sliders:
1. Add 50 more buyers → "Market Imbalance ⚖️ Buyers outnumber sellers by 50:1"
2. Prices start rising → "Price Rising! 📈 Demand exceeds supply!"
3. Price history chart shows the spike
4. Chart shows demand curve shifting right

### During Trading:
- Golden lines connect traders
- Price labels show: "$45.20\n+$8.3 surplus"
- Particles burst
- Occasionally: "💰 Trade Creates Value! This trade at $45.20 created $8.30 in total surplus!"
