      How this works & data sources

        Welcome to synthetic America

        Every county here is filled with simulated people drawn to match the real county: ages, sexes, races, education, jobs and pay by industry, health, and how the county voted. About one synthetic person stands in for every thousand real ones.

        1 · WatchReplay January 2016 to the latest release. Each month the twin is re-anchored to the real BLS numbers for the nation and all 50 states plus DC.
        2 · ExploreClick a county to meet its residents. Follow one through the pandemic.
        3 · PlayFork the timeline and change history: UI policy, sector shocks, regional shocks.
